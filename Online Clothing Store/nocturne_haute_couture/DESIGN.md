## Brand & Style

This design system channels an avant-garde runway presence, fusing the austere architectural minimalism of Saint Laurent and Balenciaga with the sleek, luminous interfaces of modern luxury digital flagships. Designed for discerning, design-literate fashion patrons, the interface evokes exclusivity, late-night high-energy intimacy, and structural discipline.

The visual style synthesizes **Dark Glass Minimalist** with **Futuristic Editorial High-Fashion**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep nocturnal planes layered with ultra-refined translucent glass panels.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-tension geometric display headers contrasted against razor-sharp, neutral utilitarian body text.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Restrained use of electric purple, neon cyan, and brushed soft gold as ambient energy markers rather than dominant fills.
- **Expansive Space:** Expansive margins, dramatic negative space, and generous tracking cultivate an elite editorial cadence.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around deep optical blackouts punctuated by high-tension photon accents and muted metallic luxury:

- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - `Canvas Midnight`: `#0B1020` — The foundational void; absorbing and deep.
  - `Surface Dark`: `#151B2E` — The base tone for cards, drawers, and modal surfaces.
  - `Glass Overlay`: `rgba(21, 27, 46, 0.12)` coupled with background blur to retain continuous depth.

- **Accent Chromatics:**
  - `Electric Purple` (`#7C3AED`): Primary callout, active states, key interactive cues, and focused focal rings.
  - `Neon Cyan` (`#22D3EE`): Secondary accent; reserved for micro-interactions, live drops, real-time stock availability, and editorial metadata tags.
  - `Soft Gold` (`#D4AF37`): Luxury highlight; dedicated exclusively to bespoke atelier pieces, runway collection seals, VIP tier signifiers, and curated editorial flags.

- **Typography & Monochrome Hierarchy:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#F8FAFC` — High legibility against dark surfaces; crisp off-white.
  - `Text Secondary`: `#94A3B8` — Slate Gray for secondary descriptions, specs, and metadata.
  - `Border Ambient`: `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)` — Defines structural edges with faint optical vibrancy.

## Typography

The typography pairs the sharp, wide-stance geometric architecture of **Sora** with the systematic clarity of **Inter**:

- **Display & Headlines (Sora):** Set with tightly controlled negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to project confidence and editorial weight. Major headlines should command significant horizontal real estate, referencing brutalist luxury lookbooks and gallery catalogs.
- **Body & Captions (Inter):** Rendered in regular and light weights (`300`/`400`) to guarantee high-density legibility for garment compositions, sizing charts, and editorial storytelling.
- **Editorial Labels (`label-caps`):** Must always be styled in uppercase with intentional tracking (`0.14em`), delivering a high-fashion runway metadata signature (e.g., `LOOK 04 / EDITION OF 50`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptable 12-column fluid grid system that prioritizes expansive white space and asymmetrical editorial blocks:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Generous side margins of `4rem` allow products to float unencumbered. Grid gutters are set to `1.5rem`. Collections cycle between 2-column full-bleed editorial imagery, 3-column lookbook spreads, and 4-column product matrices.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid scaling with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Outer margin collapses to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters. Product cards primarily adopt single-column staggered looks or balanced 2-column browse grids.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl` and higher multiples) between editorial sections prevents digital clutter, mirroring high-end print magazines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent material tiers and delicate chromatic luminescence rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers & Frosted Glass:**
  - Base level: Solid `#0B1020`.
  - Floating Level 1 (Product Cards, Navigation Bar): Background color `rgba(21, 27, 46, 0.12)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, surrounded by a hairline `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`.
  - Floating Level 2 (Flyout Carts, Drawers, Lightboxes): Background color `rgba(21, 27, 46, 0.35)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, with a dual-layer hairline border: `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.35)`.
- **Atmospheric Glow & Shadows:**
  - Standard Elevation: Shadows are soft and tinted: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(11, 16, 32, 0.7)`.
  - Active / Hover Glow: Under interactive focus, cards and action triggers project a diffused ambient aura: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.3)`. High-tier luxury triggers occasionally swap to `0 0 20px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is anchored on smooth, contemporary luxury curvature:
- **Universal Radius:** Standard containers, modal dialogs, search inputs, and visual product cards enforce a distinct `20px` corner radius (`rounded-lg` token mapping) for a seamless fluid silhouette.
- **Sub-elements:** Micro tags, chips, and nested inner controls utilize half-scale radius (`8px` to `10px`).
- **Interactive Buttons:** Fully rounded continuous pill shapes or uniform `20px` soft capsules, maintaining a tactile, organic contrast against stark rectilinear editorial photography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag / Checkout):** Solid `#7C3AED` background with `#F8FAFC` typography, or high-contrast `#F8FAFC` background with `#0B1020` typography for stark editorial presence. Border radius: `20px`. Transition duration: 250ms with subtle scale transform (`scale(1.02)`) and ambient purple glow on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Wishlist / Quick View):** Transparent backdrop with `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.35)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.12)` with `rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.5)` border.
- **Luxury / VIP Action:** Deep midnight background encased in `1px solid #D4AF37`, typography in `#D4AF37`. Accompanied by soft golden radiance on active states.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Framed with `20px` rounded corners and `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`. Background fill consists of `rgba(21, 27, 46, 0.12)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratios fixed at `3:4` or `4:5` vertical fashion cuts. Images bleed cleanly to the top, left, and right, conforming to the outer corner curves.
- **Meta Overlay:** Garment title (`Sora`, `headline-sm`), price and collection indicator (`Inter`, `label-caps`), and dynamic availability flags (Neon Cyan `#22D3EE` indicator dots).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Floating glass containers (`rgba(21, 27, 46, 0.2)`), `20px` corner radius, `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to `#22D3EE` with a subtle electric field glow (`0 0 16px rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.2)`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Off:** Background `rgba(21, 27, 46, 0.4)`, text `#94A3B8`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`.
- **State On:** Background `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #7C3AED`.

### Navigation & Header
- **Global Sticky Bar:** Translucent glass ribbon floating `1.5rem` below canvas top on desktop, radius `20px`. Background: `rgba(11, 16, 32, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`. Hairline border: `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.18)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Geometry:** Radii match parent hierarchy (checkboxes `6px`, radio buttons pure circle). Unchecked state uses `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.3)`. Checked state fills with `#7C3AED` featuring a `#F8FAFC` icon glyph.
