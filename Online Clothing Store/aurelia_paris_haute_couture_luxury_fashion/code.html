The Paris Discipline

Craftsmanship Beyond Time

Founded in the 8th arrondissement of Paris, Aurelia pairs historic hand-tailoring techniques with radical geometric modernism. Every garment is conceived not for transient seasons, but as an architectural permanent piece for the modern collector.

We source strictly from century-old family mills across Biella, Italy and Lyon, France. Our zero-waste cutting patterns ensure each limited drop honors both fabric integrity and ecological responsibility.
