Heritage & Philosophy

The Philosophy of Nocturnal Luxury

Born within an 18th-century private mansion on Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré in Paris, NOIR & GOLD was conceived to strip away theatrical distractions and place unconditional focus on pure product, rare heat, and liquid gold pairings.

Expanding subsequently into discreet enclaves in Mayfair, London, and Tokyo’s Ginza district, every dining salon remains strictly capped at 28 covers per service. We hold an enduring commitment: uncompromising reverence for biodiversity, seasonal micro-farms, and century-old viticulture.
