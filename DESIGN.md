## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal engineering ethos: the disciplined intersection of Swiss typographical precision and high-performance development environments. It treats a developer’s curriculum vitae not as marketing collateral, but as an architectural document—deliberate, structural, and unhurried. 

The aesthetic repudiates synthetic, oversaturated AI-generation markers such as generic purple-blue neon glows, hyper-radiant gradients, and decorative noise. Instead, it draws from tangible developer artifacts: physical technical manuals, high-end IDE theme design, architectural blueprints, and precision-calibrated instrumentation.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Audience:** Principal engineers, staff architects, engineering leadership, and discerning technical founders seeking uncompromising craftsmanship and demonstrable systems impact.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, quiet, intellectually rigorous, and structured. 
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute confidence in systems competence, architectural clarity, and meticulous attention to detail.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in deep obsidian slate, engineered to reduce visual fatigue while maintaining sharp typographical definition. Color is deployed strictly as semantic instrumentation rather than decoration.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0d0f12`):** Primary structural void; absorbs light and anchors deep spatial contrast.
- **Sub-surface / Tier 1 (`#14171d`):** Elevated structural blocks, code viewports, and timeline panels.
- **Surface Accent / Tier 2 (`#1c2028`):** Interactive card backgrounds, active states, and hovered matrix cells.
- **Structural Lines (`#262d38`):** Crisp 1px hairline delimiters; defines architectural separation without heavy visual weight.

### Typography & Readout
- **High-Contrast Ivory (`#f8fafc`):** Primary headings, core metrics, and active technical signatures.
- **Warm Reading Neutral (`#e2e8f0`):** Body paragraphs, structural narratives, and historical digests.
- **Muted Technical Grey (`#94a3b8` / `#64748b`):** Metadata, inline code framing, timestamps, and secondary captions.

### Deliberate Engineering Accents
- **Warm Ochre / Amber (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`):** Primary highlight instrument. Signifies critical architecture milestones, active status toggles, impact percentages, and core expertise.
- **Deep Forest Sage / Technical Emerald (`#10b981` / `#059669`):** Secondary state indicator. Encodes verified production deployments, uptime metrics, stability indices, and system tags.
- **Print / CV Export Mode:** On export media (`@media print`), colors invert cleanly to high-contrast monochrome with ochre structural anchors over pure `#ffffff` archival paper stock.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an asymmetric dual-engine system:
1. **Geist Sans:** Modern, neutral, high-density geometric structural prose. Handles human narratives, strategic career syntheses, titles, and structural headlines with optical balance.
2. **JetBrains Mono:** High-legibility technical monospace engine. Assigned to code snippets, chronological parameters, architectural badges, metric readouts, and systemic telemetry.

### Typographic Principles
- Monospace labels must always leverage tabular alignment and strict character spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` on uppercase identifiers).
- Numerical metrics must never sit in standard sans-serif; they should render via `JetBrains Mono` to emphasize quantitative precision.
- Paragraph lines are restricted to a maximum measure of 68 characters to ensure effortless structural parsing during intensive technical reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

The architectural layout utilizes a structured 12-column Swiss modular grid that shifts dynamically between interactive viewport presentation and a standard DIN A4 / US-Letter physical print ratio.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rules
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout. A fixed 4-column contextual rail anchors the developer signature, status beacons, and direct contact protocol. The remaining 8 columns house the dynamic technical timeline, capability matrix, and benchmark evidence.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 2rem gutters. Sticky sidebars collapse into a disciplined summary header followed by standard full-width structural blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column fluid stack. Margins compress to 1.25rem, retaining clear visual anchors through explicit hairline borders and vertical rule lines.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence adheres to a strict 4px/8px baseline grid. Content blocks separate across large system tiers (`space-2xl` / 4rem) to guarantee visual rest, while related metric indicators group tightly (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to reflect mechanical unity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through planar layering and tonal surface transitions rather than blurred shadows. 

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Void):** Flat `#0d0f12`. Structural foundation of the entire curriculum vitae.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Sub-sections):** `#14171d` with 1px border of `#262d38`. Used for technical timeline entries, thesis callouts, and code viewports.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Modules):** `#1c2028` with 1px border of `#333b48`. Employs a single, ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`. No ambient colored glow or neon drop-shadows are permitted.

### The Hairline Discipline
All separations rely on structural 1px hairlines. Rather than floating cards in ambiguous space, modules snap together along explicit coordinate lines, evoking an engineering instrument or technical workstation.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft-industrial corner philosophy. Pure sharp (0px) corners introduce unnecessary optical tension, whereas exaggerated curves dilute technical gravitas. 

- **Containers & Surfaces:** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners that feel precision-machined.
- **Tags, System Badges & Code Capsules:** `0.125rem` (2px) to `0.25rem` (4px). Never fully pill-shaped.
- **Buttons & Control Inputs:** `0.25rem` (4px). Consistent with chassis geometry.
- **Status Indicators:** Micro-geometric squares (6px × 6px) or precise circles (6px) with zero border radius distortion.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Export CV / Commit Key):** Background `#f59e0b`, text `#0d0f12`, weight `500`, font `JetBrains Mono`. Height: 36px, padding: 0 16px. Sharp hover transition: shifts to `#d97706`. No rounded pill outlines.
- **Secondary Action (Source Blueprint / Raw Diff):** Background `#14171d`, border `1px solid #262d38`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover: border shifts to `#94a3b8`, background transitions to `#1c2028`.
- **System Command Link:** Text-only in `#94a3b8` accompanied by terminal chevron `→`. Hover: shifts to `#f59e0b` with a 2px horizontal translate.

### Technical System Tags & Badges
- Displayed exclusively in `JetBrains Mono` at `label-caps` (11px).
- Structural styling: Background `#14171d`, border `1px solid #262d38`, text `#94a3b8`.
- **Active / Specialized Tag:** Border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`, text `#f59e0b`, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.05)`.
- **Verified Production Tag:** Border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`, text `#10b981`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.05)`.

### Timeline Architecture
- A continuous 1px vertical hairline rule in `#262d38` guides the career path.
- **Timeline Nodes:** 7px solid squares on the hairline axis. Default: `#1c2028` with `#64748b` border. Current / Active role: `#f59e0b` center with an amber-tinted focus tick.
- **Card Content:** Unboxed, relying entirely on the vertical axis and clear typographic scale (`headline-md` for roles, `label-caps` for duration and organization).

### Capability Matrix
- A structured tabular grid showing competencies (e.g., Distributed Systems, Memory Management, Protocol Design).
- Cells feature horizontal capacity indicators rendered as segmented monospace bars: `[■■■■■□□]`, pairing visual clarity with terminal nostalgia.

### Project Impact Metrics
- Two-tier informational displays: Large monospace numerical readouts (`metric-display`, e.g., `-42ms`, `99.999%`, `10x`) anchored by concise, 2-line Geist Sans descriptors (`body-sm`) in `#94a3b8`.
- Anchored within Tier 1 containers with subtle amber or emerald corner accent lines (2px width).

### Direct Contact Protocols
- Modeled after terminal environment variables or key-value registries:
  - Left column: `label-caps` muted grey (`KEY`, `GPG_FINGERPRINT`, `SIGNAL`, `MATRIX`).
  - Right column: `code-inline` ivory selectable text with one-click copy feedback.
- Visual confirmation triggers an ephemeral monospace label transition from `READY` to `COPIED`.

### Print / Presentation Mode Adaptation
- When invoked or exported to PDF, component backgrounds transition to transparent, borders convert to crisp `#cbd5e1`, and text switches to deep carbon `#0f172a` for archival legibility.
