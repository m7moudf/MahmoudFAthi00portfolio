## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a celestial, ultra-premium cinematic experience inspired by deep cosmos, the ethereal luminescence of the Northern Lights, and high-end theater projection. It targets discerning cinephiles, industry insiders, and visual purists seeking an immersive discovery environment rather than a utilitarian media repository.

The visual style synthesizes **Futuristic Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- **75% Cosmic Void**: An abyssal, velvety dark canvas allows media keyframes, poster art, and luminous accents to command absolute focus.
- **15% Ethereal Architecture**: Deep teal elevated surfaces and translucent frosted layers anchor navigation, trays, and modals.
- **10% Aurora Luminescence**: Precise, chromatic accents in aurora green, electric violet, and neon lime signify activity, state transitions, premium tiers, and curated focal points.
- **Micro-Atmospherics**: Subtle chromatic rim-lighting, diffused specular blurs, and glass surfaces simulate optical lenses and deep night skies.

## Colors

The palette balances profound darkness with vibrant, aurora-charged light signatures.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Interactive (`#36E0B0` - Aurora Green)**: Reserved for key callouts, active navigation indicators, primary play triggers, progress states, and selection states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8B5CF6` - Electric Violet)**: Denotes curated editorial selections, VIP screenings, genre tags, and atmospheric backlight gradients.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C7FF6B` - Neon Lime)**: High-energy beacon used sparingly for critical badges (e.g., 4K HDR, IMAX Enhanced, Premiere status), limited-time releases, and active live streams.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#080D18` - Midnight Navy)**: The root background canvas that absorbs ambient light and prevents eye strain.
- **Elevated Neutral (`#123C3A` - Deep Teal)**: Tonal base for structural containers, panels, elevated cards, and frosted overlays.
- **Border Structural (`#253443`)**: Low-contrast boundary tone for hairline dividers, input wrappers, and framing elements.
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - `Ice White` (`#F1F5F9`): Primary headings, titles, high-contrast labels, and primary icons.
  - `Muted Silver` (`#94A3B8`): Metadata, runtimes, synopses, secondary labels, and dormant icons.

### Color Distribution
Apply the 75-15-10 ratio strictly. Background voids must dominate the viewport. Avoid full-bleed neon containers; neon colors should manifest solely through text, iconography, thin borders, hairline glowing underlines, and radial backdrop blurs.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cinematic rhythm through high technical precision. 

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its angular, futuristic geometry and optical weight. Used for billboard movie titles, cinematic hero headers, rail categories, and high-impact numerical statistics. Apply tight letter-spacing for large titles to preserve visual tension.
- **Body & Continuous Text (Geist)**: Chosen for neutral neutrality, pristine screen rendering at smaller sizes, and technical clarity. Carries film synopsis, production details, actor bios, and setting options.
- **Eyebrows & Technical Labels (Space Grotesk Uppercase)**: Used for technical badges (e.g., `4K DOLBY VISION`, `AUDIO: ATMOS`, `PREMIERE`), metadata categories, and contextual navigation labels. Must always leverage uppercase styling paired with tracked letter-spacing (`0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system paired with horizontal sliding carousels ("cinematic rails") that intentionally break side gutters to imply infinite discovery.

### Grid & Margins
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout, 48px outer canvas margin (`margin`), 24px column gutters (`gutter`). Max content container width is capped at 1680px for wide displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout, 32px outer canvas margin (`margin-tablet`), 16px column gutters (`gutter-sm`).
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout, 20px outer margin (`margin-mobile`), 12px column gutters.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rail Bleed**: Movie trays align their primary card to the layout margin, allowing trailing cards to bleed past the right margin.
- **Generous Canvas Separation**: Maintain ample vertical spacing between distinct content rails (`space-xl` to 4rem) to let individual artworks breathe without visual clash.
- **Component Tightness**: Content nested within cards or overlay pills uses tight internal padding (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to maintain density.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics physical cinema viewing: layered light shining through tinted, anti-reflective optical glass against a light-absorbing backdrop.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface (Z-0)**: Solid Midnight Navy (`#080D18`).
2. **Elevated Surface / Glass (Z-1)**: Deep Teal with alpha transparency (`rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.45)`), backed by a `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and framed by a 1px border of `rgba(37, 52, 67, 0.6)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Z-2)**: Frosted Teal-Black (`rgba(8, 13, 24, 0.75)`), backdrop blur `32px`, highlighted with a subtle top edge specular gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(54, 224, 176, 0.15) 0%, rgba(37, 52, 67, 0.3) 100%)`).
4. **Modals & Video Drawers (Z-3)**: Pure deep dark backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(40px)` with `rgba(8, 13, 24, 0.85)`).

### Atmospheric Glow & Shadows
Standard black drop shadows are prohibited on interactive cards. Instead, use colored, diffuse ambient glow signatures:
- **Default Elevation Shadow**: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Active Focus / Aurora Glow**: `0 0 40px -8px rgba(54, 224, 176, 0.35), 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Special Tier / Violet Glow**: `0 0 48px -10px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a consistent radius scale calibrated between 12px and 16px to reflect high-precision industrial hardware and sleek, modern glass screens.

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded-md` / 8px)**: Reserved for minor inline items, nested control toggles, and metadata tags.
- **Standard Component Radius (`rounded-lg` / 12px to 16px)**: Applied to movie poster cards, interactive controls, media trays, search inputs, and content drawers.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-xl` / 24px)**: Applied to high-level panels, modal viewports, and primary hero banners.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for contextual action chips, playback state toggles, and status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Watch Now, Resume)**: Solid Aurora Green background (`#36E0B0`), text in `#080D18` (`Space Grotesk`, medium/semi-bold). Soft outer green luminescence on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(54, 224, 176, 0.4)`), scale `1.02`.
- **Secondary Action (Add to List, Details)**: Frosted Deep Teal surface (`rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.4)`), 1px border (`#253443`), text in `#F1F5F9`. Hover state shifts border to `rgba(54, 224, 176, 0.5)`.
- **Icon / Ghost Play Trigger**: Circular pill (`rounded-full`), frosted dark backdrop with `1px` translucent border. Inner icon lights up in `#36E0B0` on focus.

### Movie & Media Cards
- **Base Structure**: 16:9 widescreen or 2:3 cinematic poster ratios with `12px` to `16px` corner radius.
- **Resting State**: Hairline border (`#253443`), subdued image with a bottom scrim gradient transitioning smoothly into `#080D18`.
- **Hover / Active State**: Smooth vertical translate (`-4px`), border illuminates to `#36E0B0`, accompanied by an ambient aurora bloom behind the card (`0 16px 36px -8px rgba(54, 224, 176, 0.25)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Format Specs (`4K`, `HDR10+`, `IMAX`)**: Ultra-compact pill, transparent background, solid hairline border (`#253443`), typography in `label-caps` (`#94A3B8`).
- **Feature Tags (Exclusive, New)**: Electric Violet surface tint (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`), border `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`, text in `#F1F5F9`.
- **Premiere / Live Status**: Neon Lime text (`#C7FF6B`), pulsing indicator dot with lime blur.

### Input Fields & Search Bar
- **Surface**: Translucent Deep Teal (`rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.35)`), backdrop blur `16px`.
- **Border**: 1px `#253443`. On focus: dynamic transition to `#36E0B0` outline with soft radial glow.
- **Typography**: Text in `#F1F5F9`, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes**: 18px rounded square (4px radius). Unchecked: `#253443` border, transparent core. Checked: Solid `#36E0B0` fill, Midnight Navy check icon.
- **Segmented Video Quality Switcher**: Deep Teal pill container. Active selection sits on an elevated frosted pod with a razor-thin `#36E0B0` accent glow.

### Custom Component: Cinematic Scrubber / Timeline
- **Track**: 4px height, `#253443` background.
- **Played Fill**: Gradient track from Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) to Aurora Green (`#36E0B0`).
- **Handle**: 14px circular glass bead with a solid Ice White center and glowing Aurora Green aura.
